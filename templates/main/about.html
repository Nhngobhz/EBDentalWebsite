Our Story

A name dental professionals have trusted since 1985

For over 40 years, EB Dental Supply has been a trusted partner for dental professionals across Cambodia and the region. We provide high-quality instruments, equipment, and consumables from the world's leading brands, ensuring that practitioners have access to the best tools for patient care.

From our home at Orussey Market in Phnom Penh, we support everything from a single hand instrument to a full clinic fit-out — backed by a team that knows the products it sells and stands behind them long after delivery.
